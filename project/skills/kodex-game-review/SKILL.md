# Kodex game prep and review

The list comes from the player: a KODEX block from their app, a pasted or attached list (New Recruit, the Warhammer 40,000 app), or a description; ask for it if there is none. Read the rules it uses with the kodex-rules-lookup skill (current Faction Pack and Wahapedia 11th edition), core-rules.md for timing, detachment-dispositions.md for Force Dispositions, and GW's Event Companion for the mission sequence. Keep what the rules say (quoted, with its source) apart from your advice (labelled as advice).

## Before a game ("I'm playing X with list Y")
Give, short and scannable, in this order:
1. **Force Dispositions** this list can lock: every disposition one of its detachments allows (detachment-dispositions.md; a detachment missing there is newer than the file: look it up). For what a disposition or mission card asks, use the card's text if the player gives it or GW publishes it; otherwise say to check the card rather than guess.
2. **Before the battle:** deployment rules, Scouts/Infiltrators, reserves and Deep Strike units, attached units, enhancements, anything chosen before the game (e.g. a Ruinator's victim, a Pledge).
3. **Each phase, what this list does:** Command (CP, start-of-round and Command-phase abilities), Movement, Shooting, Charge, Fight, End of turn: per phase the rules and stratagems that fire there, named with their unit, one line each.
4. **Once per battle / once per round** things, so they are not forgotten.
5. **Against this opponent**: their army rule and the few rules that change how this list plays (e.g. -1 to hit, Feel No Pain, Lone Operative), looked up and quoted briefly.
Stay under a phone screen or two. No generic tactics.

## After a game ("we lost 40-60, my Lord died turn 2...")
1. Ask at most two short questions if the story is unclear (mission, who went first, the score).
2. **What the rules say** about any moment the player is unsure was played right: the ruling, as the Project's instructions describe.
3. **What went well, what cost the game:** concrete, tied to units and turns the player described. Advice, labelled.
4. **For next time:** two or three changes in play (not in the list unless asked); if asked about the list, suggestions from the faction's current units and detachments, with points only where a current GW source gives them.
Be honest and kind; no filler.
