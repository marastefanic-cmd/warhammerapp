ARMY AND DETACHMENT RULES
Martial Ka’tah
Start of your Command phase

At the start of your Command phase, friendly ADEPTUS CUSTODES units with this ability are readied. While a friendly unit is readied, you can select one of the ka’tah abilities presented below for that unit to use. If you do, that unit is no longer readied.

Each ka’tah ability will tell you when that ka’tah ability can be used, and the effects that ka’tah ability has on the unit using it.

We recommend placing a token next to each readied unit, removing it when that unit is no longer readied.

Favoured Ka’tah

All detachments tagged ‘Unique: Shield Host’ state a Favoured Ka’tah. If you selected one of these detachments, the ka’tah ability that is the Favoured Ka’tah of that detachment grants the additional effect listed in that detachment.

Example: If you selected the Solar Watch detachment, when you select the Calistus ka’tah ability for a readied unit, that unit has +2" M in addition to the normal effect of Calistus.

Conservai

KA’TAH ABILITY

WHEN: Your Command phase.

TARGET: One friendly readied unit.

EFFECT: Until the end of the turn:

Being engaged/battle-shocked does not prevent your unit from being eligible to start an action.
Starting an action does not prevent your unit from being eligible to shoot.

KA’TAH ABILITY

EFFECT: When your unit makes a normal/advance/fall-back move, your unit can move through all types of model.
